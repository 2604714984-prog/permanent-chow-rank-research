# Adversarial review: coordinate first-order eight-term floor

The proof is not a new local computation. It applies the already verified
one-frame bound `e+p+v<=6` to an arbitrary number `q` of components. The global
incidence lower bound remains 48 because the target always has 24 nonzero
matching coordinates.

Repeated factors remain included through `v`. A target in exactly one envelope
is charged to `v` only after the order-zero coefficientwise cancellation
argument; no naive cross-component source-sharing assumption is used.

The theorem is restricted to regular coordinate initial frames and a first
nonzero term at order one. It does not imply unrestricted `mu(6,4)>=8`, does
not prove existence at eight components, and makes no ordinary-rank,
border-rank, or novelty claim.
