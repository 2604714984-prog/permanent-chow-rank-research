# N6-091: excluding the \(x_A=73,\ldots,76\) shortening plateau

N6-089 leaves \(x_A,f_A\le76\). We descend through
\(s=76,75,74,73\). At each step the exact product-shadow minimum remains

\[
 m_s=90.                                                     \tag{1.1}
\]

The complementary fifteen-set literal floor is \(366-s\), and the seven-set
literal floor is \(206-s\). The same eleven-state defect-three envelope from
N6-081 applies. Ten states have \(t_2\le14\); their old prolongation gaps only
increase by \(80-s\). The unique survivor has

\[
 \varepsilon=(0^7),\qquad \kappa_2=0,\qquad
 d_2=105,\qquad t_2=15.                                     \tag{1.2}
\]

Quadratic directness gives a literal middle space \(L_A\) of dimension
\(140\). If any \(\alpha_i\le2\), the inherited upper cap is \(458\), while

\[
 \dim(E_3+L_A)=400+140-s=464,465,466,467                     \tag{1.3}
\]

for \(s=76,75,74,73\). Thus all seven \(\alpha_i\) equal three, the seven
quadratic images are direct over one common \(W_{15}\), and their permanent
intersection has dimension \(90\).

N6-090 extends the \(s\to90\) equality plane to the partitioned
\(80\to90\) product locus. The actual endpoint exclusion N6-083 then applies
without change. In the separated branch, the exact cubic permanent
intersection has dimension at most \(60<s\). In the all-singular branch,
the four active product rows cannot carry a rank-six factor frame. Both
branches are impossible.

Descending induction therefore gives the strict conclusion

\[
 \boxed{x_A\le72,\qquad f_A\le72}                            \tag{1.4}
\]

for every residual seven-set in a global \(b=34\) survivor. The next layer
is genuinely different: \(m_{72}=89\), so the present shadow-\(90\) product
extension no longer applies. This result does not exclude global \(b=34\),
prove ordinary lower \(29\), or imply a border-rank bound.
