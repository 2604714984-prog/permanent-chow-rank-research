# Forced Hessian witnesses are tautologically orthogonal to `K2`

## Status

`DECISIVE NEGATIVE RESULT: THE HESSIAN K5 SUBSPACE CANNOT OBSTRUCT THE 2/5 ENDPOINT.`

The completed same-row Hessian witness space `W^all` is nonzero on every
remaining Packet-A candidate, but its inverse-coefficient pairing with `K2`
vanishes identically.  This is not a consequence of the endpoint equality;
it follows directly by differentiating the defining quadratic relation
`A2 x=0`.

## 1. Label conventions

For a term

\[
 T_i=\prod_{r=1}^7\ell_{i,r},
 \qquad
 \ell_{i,r}=\sum_{u,b}a_{i,r,u,b}x_{u,b},
\]

write a degree-two labelled coordinate as `(i,{r,s})` and its complementary
degree-five coordinate as `(i,hat{r,s})`.  The aggregate quadratic map is

\[
 A_2e_{i,\{r,s\}}=\ell_{i,r}\ell_{i,s}.
\]

Thus `x in K2=ker A2` means the polynomial identity

\[
 A_2x
 =\sum_i\sum_{r<s}x_{i,\{r,s\}}\ell_{i,r}\ell_{i,s}=0.
\tag{1}
\]

Let `P:M5 -> M2` send `(i,hat{r,s})` to `(i,{r,s})`, and let `D` have the
external coefficient `c_i` on every one of the 21 labels of term `i`.  The
implemented inverse-coefficient pairing is

\[
 x^{\mathsf T}D^{-1}Pw,
 \qquad x\in K_2,\quad w\in K_5.
\tag{2}
\]

## 2. Distinct-column witnesses

For one row `u` and distinct columns `b<d`, the forced Hessian witness has
coordinates

\[
 W_{b,d,u}[i,\widehat{r,s}]
 =c_i\bigl(
 a_{i,r,u,b}a_{i,s,u,d}
 +a_{i,r,u,d}a_{i,s,u,b}
 \bigr).
\tag{3}
\]

Applying `P` replaces the complementary label by `{r,s}`.  Applying `D^-1`
cancels `c_i`, with no residual coefficient or term-label permutation.  Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
 x^{\mathsf T}D^{-1}PW_{b,d,u}
 &=\sum_i\sum_{r<s}x_{i,\{r,s\}}
 \bigl(a_{i,r,u,b}a_{i,s,u,d}
      +a_{i,r,u,d}a_{i,s,u,b}\bigr)\\
 &=\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_{u,b}\partial x_{u,d}}
   (A_2x).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]

The right-hand side is zero by (1).

## 3. Same-column witnesses and the factor two

For `b=d`, differentiating a product of two linear forms twice in the same
variable gives

\[
 \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_{u,b}^2}
 (\ell_{i,r}\ell_{i,s})
 =2a_{i,r,u,b}a_{i,s,u,b}.
\]

The completed witness uses exactly this normalization:

\[
 W_{b,b,u}[i,\widehat{r,s}]
 =2c_i a_{i,r,u,b}a_{i,s,u,b}.
\tag{5}
\]

Therefore

\[
 x^{\mathsf T}D^{-1}PW_{b,b,u}
 =\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_{u,b}^2}(A_2x)=0.
\tag{6}
\]

Equations (4) and (6) cover all 196 columns of `W^all` and prove

\[
 \boxed{K_2^{\mathsf T}D^{-1}P W^{\rm all}=0}
\tag{7}
\]

for every factor packet, independently of the permanent target and
independently of `ker B subset im C`.

## 4. Matrix form of the tautology

Let `h_(b,d,u)` be the coordinate Hessian functional on quadratic
polynomials, including the same-column normalization in (5).  The coordinate
calculation above is the stronger matrix identity

\[
 D^{-1}PW_{b,d,u}=A_2^{\mathsf T}h_{b,d,u}.
\tag{8}
\]

Consequently

\[
 D^{-1}P W^{\rm all}\subseteq\operatorname{im}A_2^{\mathsf T}
 =(\ker A_2)^\perp.
\tag{9}
\]

For a true permanent identity, the same-row derivatives of the target vanish,
so the witness columns also satisfy `W^all subset K5`.  Combining this fact
with (9) shows that `W^all` lies in the right radical of the restricted 2/5
pairing by construction.

This explains why the endpoint quotient analysis could not turn a nonzero
Hessian witness into a contradiction.  Nonzero `W^all`, nonzero `K2`, and
zero pairing between them are perfectly compatible and in fact mandatory.

## 5. The next genuinely effective invariant

The relevant obstruction quotient is not generated by `W^all`.  Define

\[
 \mathcal O_{2/5}
 =\operatorname{im}\left(
 K_5\xrightarrow{\ D^{-1}P\ }M_2
 \longrightarrow M_2/\operatorname{im}A_2^{\mathsf T}
 \right).
\tag{10}
\]

The endpoint relation condition is equivalent to

\[
 \mathcal O_{2/5}=0.
\]

Equation (9) says only that the Hessian-generated subspace maps to zero in
this quotient.  A genuinely effective next invariant must therefore produce
a relation

\[
 z\in K_5
 \quad\text{with}\quad
 D^{-1}Pz\notin\operatorname{im}A_2^{\mathsf T},
\tag{11}
\]

or prove directly that the image (10) has positive dimension.

The smallest plausible source is a cross-target relation among different
off-row Hessian blocks or a higher Koszul/Pluecker syzygy of the permanent
targets whose coefficient vector is not the transpose evaluation of any
quadratic functional.  Merely adding more same-row derivatives, changing
torus blocks, or taking linear combinations of the 196 existing witnesses
cannot work: all remain inside `im A2^T` by (8).

Thus the next Packet-A gate is the transverse quotient rank

\[
 \operatorname{rank}\left(
 K_5\to M_2/\operatorname{im}A_2^{\mathsf T}
 \right),
\]

after factoring out every derivative-generated `K5` relation.  Until this
rank is proved positive, Packet A, the ordinary lower bound 50, and every
