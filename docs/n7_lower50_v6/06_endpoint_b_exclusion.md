# v6 Module 06 — endpoint B exclusion audit

Audit the mixed packet with seven rank-six equality terms and forty-two
rank-seven graph complements.

## B49-01 — freeze the direct basis

Use the seven rank-six planes and one graph complement to form a direct decomposition of `V`; retain `s=1` and `s=2` local algebras separately.

**Decisive output:** `B-DIRECT-BASIS-FROZEN`.

## B49-02 — derive the 210 isomorphisms

Recompute local middle dimensions and prove both `R3` and `R4` restrictions to the eight basis blocks are isomorphisms.

**Decisive output:** `B-MIDDLE-ISOMORPHISMS`.

## B49-03 — write every other graph plane intrinsically

Represent `L_t` as the graph of `N_t:L0->A` and verify orientation-independent formulas for all restriction blocks.

**Decisive output:** `GRAPH-MAP-ORIENTATION`.

## B49-04 — derive `rank N_t>=5`

Translate the pair-span floor with `L0` into the exact rank bound, including noncanonical graph coordinates.

**Decisive output:** `GRAPH-RANK-FLOOR`.

## B49-05 — kill components sourced in rank-six blocks

Multiply their unique cubic lifts by a codeword supported at `L0`; use the invertible graph projection and Boolean no-socle lemma.

**Decisive output:** `RANK6-SOURCE-COMPONENTS-ZERO`.

## B49-06 — define the transverse linear space `W`

Prove `W=sum im P_tc=im N_t^*` and `dim W>=5` with the correct dual orientation.

**Decisive output:** `TRANSVERSE-W-FROZEN`.

## B49-07 — prove the Boolean quotient lemma

Give a complete proof that for any at-least-five-dimensional `W` in a seven-variable Boolean algebra, `W(A_t)_3=(A_t)_4`. The argument must handle arbitrary `W`, not only coordinate subspaces.

**Decisive output:** `BOOLEAN-WA3-EQUALS-A4`.

## B49-08 — independently classify the two-generator quotient

After quotienting by `W`, prove degree four vanishes by classifying the restrictions of the seven square-zero coordinate forms to a space of dimension at most two.

**Decisive output:** `BOOLEAN-QUOTIENT-INDEPENDENT-PROOF`.

## B49-09 — kill the graph-source component

Use perfect degree-(3,4) pairing and the Boolean quotient lemma to show every cubic annihilated by `W` is zero.

**Decisive output:** `GRAPH-SOURCE-COMPONENT-ZERO`.

## B49-10 — force total projection zero

Combine all eight source blocks and prove the projection `R3 -> (A_t)_3` is the zero map.

**Decisive output:** `B-PROJECTION-ZERO`.

## B49-11 — prove individual cubic surjectivity

Use duality and the cubic-intersection-zero theorem to make the same map surjective.

**Decisive output:** `B-INDIVIDUAL-SURJECTIVITY`.

## B49-12 — cover all forty-two complements

Show the contradiction applies separately to every nonbasis graph plane without assuming tail synchronization or a common graph.

**Decisive output:** `ARBITRARY-GRAPH-COVERAGE`.

## B49-13 — reconcile active low-layer survivors

Explain why transposition and join survivors from the active branch do not satisfy the complete endpoint hypotheses and therefore do not refute the proof.

**Decisive output:** `B-SURVIVOR-SCOPE-RECONCILED`.

## B49-14 — endpoint B decision

Return pass only if arbitrary graph complements, both rank-six types, and all boundary orientations are covered.

**Decisive output:** `ENDPOINT-B-AUDIT-PASS`.
