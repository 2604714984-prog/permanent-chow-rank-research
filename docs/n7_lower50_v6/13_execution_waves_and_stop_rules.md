to keep hardware busy.

Complete Modules 00 and 01. Stop immediately on a section-cap defect.

**Decisive output:** `WAVE1-DECIDED`.

## WX-07 — stop blind family expansion

**Decisive output:** `NO-BLIND-EXPANSION`.

## WX-08 — stop duplicate proof infrastructure

No scheduler, database, registry, generalized CAS, or second evidence platform.

**Decisive output:** `MINIMAL-INFRASTRUCTURE`.

## WX-09 — stop on exact survivor

An exact endpoint or 50-term survivor takes immediate priority over further exclusions.

**Decisive output:** `SURVIVOR-PRIORITY`.

## WX-10 — stop superseded routes

Do not rebuild false quadratic surjectivity, automatic plain Packet-A induction, or closed common-graph B1.

**Decisive output:** `SUPERSEDED-ROUTES-BLOCKED`.

## WX-11 — resource discipline

Large computations require a theorem-defined finite state space, precomputed memory bound, deterministic checkpoints, and exact promotion path.

**Decisive output:** `COMPUTE-GATE`.

## WX-12 — replanning threshold

Replan only at one of the statuses listed in the main index.

**Decisive output:** `V6-CONTINUOUS-EXECUTION`.
