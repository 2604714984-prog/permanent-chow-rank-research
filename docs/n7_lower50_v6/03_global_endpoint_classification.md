# v6 Module 03 — global filtration and endpoint classification

Rebuild the transition from local slope ten to the two exhaustive 49-term
factor packets. This module must not assume either endpoint geometry in
advance.

## GE-01 — audit apolar dimension identities

Prove `dim R_d = dim H_d - binom(7,d)^2` with the correct quotient `C=S/J` and `R=I/J`.

**Decisive output:** `APOLAR-DIMENSIONS-PROVED`.

## GE-02 — source-check quadratic generation

Verify Shafiei's theorem and the deductions `R3=C1 R2`, `R4=C1^2 R2`, `E2^(1)=E3`, and `E3^(1)=E4`.

**Decisive output:** `SHAFIEI-SOURCE-RECONCILED`.

## GE-03 — audit global polarization kernels

Prove that the kernels of the two quotient polarizations on `H4` and `H3` are exactly `E4` and `E3`.

**Decisive output:** `GLOBAL-KERNELS-EXACT`.

## GE-04 — audit the factor-span filtration

Define `W_i`, quotient dimensions `d_i`, and the filtered image increments. Prove earlier terms vanish after projection.

**Decisive output:** `FILTRATION-WELL-DEFINED`.

## GE-05 — identify local diagonal blocks

Show that each filtered increment is the actual local symbol from Module 02, with no missing quotient or intersection term.

**Decisive output:** `LOCAL-TO-GLOBAL-IDENTIFICATION`.

## GE-06 — audit rectangular Sylvester

Reconstruct the middle factorization and prove the upper bound on `h3+h4`, including all defect signs.

**Decisive output:** `SYLVESTER-UPPER-BOUND`.

## GE-07 — build an exact slack ledger

Record local slope slack, filtration slack, and Sylvester slack. At `N=49`, prove their sum is zero.

**Decisive output:** `N49-ZERO-SLACK-IDENTITY`.

## GE-08 — justify arbitrary reordering

Prove that equality for the global identity applies to every ordering and therefore constrains each term when placed first.

**Decisive output:** `ORDER-INVARIANCE-PROVED`.

## GE-09 — exclude the rank-five alternative

Check the divisibility argument after putting the unique rank-five term first and verify no zero-increment low-rank exception exists.

**Decisive output:** `RANK5-ENDPOINT-EXCLUDED`.

## GE-10 — solve the rank-six count equation

Prove rank-six terms are direct, support type `s=1,2`, and their count is exactly zero or seven.

**Decisive output:** `RANK6-COUNT-CLASSIFIED`.

## GE-11 — derive endpoint A

Prove the all-rank-seven planes represent a simple rank-seven 7-multilinear matroid for every subset.

**Decisive output:** `ENDPOINT-A-DERIVED`.

## GE-12 — derive endpoint B

Prove the seven rank-six planes form a direct 42-space and every remaining rank-seven plane is a graph complement with pairwise span at least twelve.

**Decisive output:** `ENDPOINT-B-DERIVED`.

## GE-13 — audit padding to 49 terms

Verify scalar splitting of a nonzero Chow term preserves nonzero summands and the field/scope assumptions.

**Decisive output:** `PADDING-LEMMA`.

## GE-14 — endpoint classification decision

Return pass only if the two packets are exhaustive under arbitrary repeated or dependent factors.

**Decisive output:** `N49-ENDPOINT-CLASSIFICATION-PASS`.
