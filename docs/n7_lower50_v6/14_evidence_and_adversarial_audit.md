# v6 Module 14 — evidence, survivor, and adversarial audit standard

Use proportionate review: strict on load-bearing claims, minimal on routine
support code.

## QA-01 — claim-scope ledger

For each promoted statement record field, characteristic, ordinary/border scope, minimality, reducedness, nonzero coefficients, and external dependencies.

**Decisive output:** `CLAIM-SCOPE-LEDGER`.

## QA-02 — source map

Every external theorem receives exact paper/version/theorem identifiers and a statement-to-use comparison.

**Decisive output:** `EXTERNAL-SOURCE-MAP`.

## QA-03 — finite computation map

Record input commit, script hash, data hash, arithmetic domain, upper/lower-bound direction, and replay command.

**Decisive output:** `FINITE-EVIDENCE-MAP`.

## QA-04 — survivor schema

A complete survivor must include all factors, coefficients, exact identity residual, derivative ranks, endpoint conditions, and field representation.

**Decisive output:** `SURVIVOR-SCHEMA`.

## QA-05 — counterexample suite

Maintain false-lemma controls for quadratic surjectivity, transpose orientation, generic-only quotient arguments, and abstract Sylvester splitting.

**Decisive output:** `COUNTEREXAMPLE-SUITE`.

## QA-06 — boundary audit

Every chart, invertible minor, torus saturation, and degeneration has an explicit complement or cited theorem.

**Decisive output:** `BOUNDARY-AUDIT`.

## QA-07 — semicontinuity audit

Every limit argument states which rank or dimension may rise or fall and why the conclusion follows.

**Decisive output:** `SEMICONTINUITY-LEDGER`.

## QA-08 — independent proof-chain review

A reviewer reconstructs the final chain without trusting task labels, PR prose, or the author's intended order.

**Decisive output:** `INDEPENDENT-CHAIN-REVIEW`.

## QA-09 — severity discipline

Use `fatal`, `major`, `minor`, or `editorial`; no confidence-only verdicts.

**Decisive output:** `SEVERITY-LEDGER`.

## QA-10 — compact final artifacts

The theorem package should retain one proof note, one dependency/audit note, compact evidence per computational lemma, and focused tests.

**Decisive output:** `COMPACT-THEOREM-PACKET`.

## QA-11 — CI receipt

A completed successful workflow at the exact promoted head is mandatory. Missing, skipped load-bearing, or running jobs are not pass.

**Decisive output:** `FINAL-CI-RECEIPT`.

## QA-12 — terminal status

Return one of `LOWER-50-PROMOTED`, `LOWER-50-EXPLICIT-GAP`, `LOWER-51-PROMOTABLE`, or `EXACT-50-TERM-SURVIVOR` as appropriate.

**Decisive output:** `V6-TERMINAL-STATUS`.
