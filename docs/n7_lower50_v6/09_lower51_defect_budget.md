# v6 Module 09 — gated 50-term near-equality defect budget

Dormant until lower 50 is promoted. For a minimal 50-term identity, the global
upper/lower comparison leaves exactly 35 units of total slack. The purpose is
to convert this into a finite structural frontier.

## N50-01 — derive the general-N formulas

Write the rectangular Sylvester upper bound and filtration lower bound for arbitrary `N`, with all defect terms.

**Decisive output:** `GENERAL-N-MIDDLE-BOUNDS`.

## N50-02 — define three nonnegative slacks

Define local slope surplus `sigma`, filtration surplus `eta`, and Sylvester defect `epsilon` so that at `N=50` their exact sum is 35.

**Decisive output:** `N50-DEFECT-IDENTITY`.

## N50-03 — compute the local surplus spectrum

For every factor rank, normal form, and quotient increment, calculate the exact minimum surplus above slope ten.

**Decisive output:** `LOCAL-SURPLUS-TABLE`.

## N50-04 — include zero-increment terms

Classify when a term contained in the previous span contributes zero increment and what equality/strictness remains.

**Decisive output:** `ZERO-INCREMENT-SPECTRUM`.

## N50-05 — derive ordering constraints

Because the factor spans may be reordered, translate the 35-unit budget into inequalities for every element and subset.

**Decisive output:** `ORDER-INVARIANT-BUDGET`.

## N50-06 — derive factor-rank count bounds

Use the surplus spectrum to bound the number of rank-five, rank-six by support type, and rank-seven terms.

**Decisive output:** `N50-RANK-COUNT-BOUNDS`.

## N50-07 — audit minimality consequences

Use the promoted lower 50 to show every 50-term identity is minimal and identify which sub-sums inherit catalectic or apolar minimality.

**Decisive output:** `N50-MINIMALITY-LEDGER`.

## N50-08 — seek new section caps

Determine whether minimality plus 50 terms yields nontrivial one-term or pair factor-span floors beyond the trivial `C6(49)` bound.

**Decisive output:** `N50-SECTION-IMPROVEMENT-DECISION`.

## N50-09 — enumerate integer defect allocations

Solve the exact finite integer problem for rank counts, quotient increments, and total slack 35.

**Decisive output:** `N50-INTEGER-FRONTIER`.

## N50-10 — attach polymatroid constraints

Filter integer allocations by representable factor-span rank functions and every-order local equality conditions.

**Decisive output:** `N50-POLYMATROID-FRONTIER`.

## N50-11 — separate direct-basis profiles

Classify packets admitting seven direct rank-seven planes, mixed direct bases, or no direct seven-plane basis.

**Decisive output:** `N50-BASIS-PROFILES`.

## N50-12 — import the conditional simple exclusion

Audit and retain the existing theorem excluding the all-rank-seven simple 50-element multilinear-matroid branch.

**Decisive output:** `SIMPLE-N50-BRANCH-STATUS`.

## N50-13 — find exact structural controls

Construct at least one abstract or Chow-realizable control for each remaining numerical packet when possible.

**Decisive output:** `N50-CONTROL-LIBRARY`.

## N50-14 — reject random-family completion

No packet is closed because random instances fail. Every surviving or excluded branch must be theorem-defined.

**Decisive output:** `N50-NO-RANDOM-CLOSURE`.

## N50-15 — freeze the finite frontier

Publish one list of all structural packet types that can consume the 35-unit budget.

**Decisive output:** `N50-DEFECT-FRONTIER-FROZEN`.

## N50-16 — defect-budget gate

Lower-51 obstruction work starts only after the finite frontier is complete or one exact 50-term survivor is found.

**Decisive output:** `N50-BUDGET-GATE-PASS`.
