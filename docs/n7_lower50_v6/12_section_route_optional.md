# v6 Module 12 — optional coordinate-section route

This is a gated high-risk lane after lower 50. It must not displace the finite
N=50 packet program unless it produces a concrete invariant.

## SR-01 — freeze the section polynomial

Set `Q=perm7|x77=0` and record its exact multigrading, apolar ideal interface, and known 63-term upper bound.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-Q-FROZEN`.

## SR-02 — state the sharp target

The useful theorem is `borderCR(Q)>=63`; distinguish it from ordinary rank and from the already known upper bound.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-TARGET-SCOPED`.

## SR-03 — inventory standard flattening ceilings

Prove which catalectic, Koszul–Young, and one-step section maps cannot reach 63.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-STANDARD-CEILINGS`.

## SR-04 — construct the bipartite-grid module

Retain the full 6-by-6 grid/apolar structure induced by deleting one coordinate rather than scalar section dimensions.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-GRID-MODULE`.

## SR-05 — minimal product-circuit invariant

Couple a minimal product circuit to first and second factor-deletion modules in a coordinate-invariant way.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-CIRCUIT-INVARIANT`.

## SR-06 — small-n calibration

Test the invariant on exact small sections where ranks are known and require correct behavior on Glynn degenerations.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-SMALL-N-PASS`.

## SR-07 — one-term cap

Compute or bound the invariant for an arbitrary Chow term after section, including dependent factors.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-ONE-TERM-CAP`.

## SR-08 — target value

Compute the permanent-section value exactly or give a rigorous lower bound.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-TARGET-VALUE`.

## SR-09 — promotion decision

Retain the route only if the ratio can prove a new theorem; otherwise freeze a quantified ceiling.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-ROUTE-DECIDED`.

## SR-10 — scope control

No section result is promoted to unrestricted lower 51 or exact 64 without a proved transfer theorem.

**Decisive output:** `SECTION-SCOPE-GATE`.
