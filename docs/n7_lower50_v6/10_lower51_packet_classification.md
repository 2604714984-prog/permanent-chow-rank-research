# v6 Module 10 — gated classification of 50-term packets

Turn the defect-budget frontier into exhaustive represented configurations,
not merely integer profiles.

## P50-01 — classify the all-rank-seven simple branch

Integrate the audited conditional exclusion and list any assumptions not forced by Module 09.

**Decisive output:** `P50-SIMPLE-R7-DECIDED`.

## P50-02 — classify parallel or repeated rank-seven planes

Analyze non-simple multilinear representations and the cost of parallel elements under minimality.

**Decisive output:** `P50-PARALLEL-BRANCH`.

## P50-03 — classify rank-six mixtures

Enumerate support types `s=1..6`, directness patterns, and permitted slope surplus.

**Decisive output:** `P50-RANK6-BRANCHES`.

## P50-04 — classify rank-five appearances

Use local surplus and ordering constraints to decide whether any rank-five term can occur in a minimal 50-term packet.

**Decisive output:** `P50-RANK5-DECISION`.

## P50-05 — classify mixed direct bases

List all possible direct decompositions of `V` by rank-six and rank-seven factor planes compatible with the budget.

**Decisive output:** `P50-MIXED-BASES`.

## P50-06 — classify no-direct-basis profiles

Use representable polymatroid inequalities to isolate packets in which no selected factor planes give a direct 49-dimensional decomposition.

**Decisive output:** `P50-NO-DIRECT-BASIS`.

## P50-07 — derive fundamental-circuit data

For every basis profile, compute the allowed ranks of restriction blocks and circuit supports.

**Decisive output:** `P50-CIRCUIT-DATA`.

## P50-08 — derive residual middle dimensions

Compute the dimensions of kernels left after restriction to a direct basis; unlike `N=49`, up to 35 residual dimensions may remain.

**Decisive output:** `P50-RESIDUAL-MIDDLE-DIMS`.

## P50-09 — classify residual algebra modules

Determine how the residual `K3,K4` spaces transform under multiplication by basis-supported codewords.

**Decisive output:** `P50-RESIDUAL-MODULE-TYPES`.

## P50-10 — handle rank-six local algebras

Retain the distinct `s=1,2` and higher-support multiplication tables where they survive.

**Decisive output:** `P50-RANK6-ALGEBRAS`.

## P50-11 — analyze boundary degenerations

Cover coincident planes, rank-drop graph maps, nonreduced limits, and zero coefficients without leaving the minimal 50-term locus.

**Decisive output:** `P50-BOUNDARIES`.

## P50-12 — search for exact survivors

On each theorem-defined branch, solve the smallest exact equations and freeze any rational or number-field survivor.

**Decisive output:** `P50-SURVIVOR-SEARCH`.

## P50-13 — validate survivor completeness

A local module or projected survivor is not a 50-term decomposition. Require factors, coefficients, identity residual, and all rank conditions.

**Decisive output:** `P50-SURVIVOR-SCHEMA`.

## P50-14 — build branch-specific negative controls

Ensure each obstruction distinguishes an actual permanent-specific condition from abstract Sylvester equality.

**Decisive output:** `P50-NEGATIVE-CONTROLS`.

## P50-15 — reuse active v5 tools selectively

Map refined Koszul, Pluecker, second-order, and identifiability tools only to branches satisfying their hypotheses.

**Decisive output:** `P50-V5-TOOL-MAP`.

## P50-16 — produce an exhaustive packet table

Every budget-feasible represented packet must appear once with a status and next invariant.

**Decisive output:** `N50-PACKET-CLASSIFICATION-COMPLETE`.

## P50-17 — decide early survivor priority

If an exact 50-term decomposition appears, suspend exclusion work and independently verify it.

**Decisive output:** `EXACT-50-TERM-SURVIVOR-OR-CONTINUE`.

## P50-18 — packet-classification gate

Module 11 begins only after the table is exhaustive.

**Decisive output:** `P50-CLASSIFICATION-GATE-PASS`.
