# Handoff delta: regular-sequence partial quotient cap

The cubic-corrected partial quotient group

```text
Tor_1^S(S/(I_2),S/(W))_3
```

satisfies

```text
dim <= (r-d)*min(q,d) <= d*(r-d)
```

whenever the `q` quadratic generators in `I_2` form a regular sequence.
This strictly enlarges the previously proved simultaneously diagonalizable
class. The independent square quadrics attain equality.

The remaining one-term gate is now:

```text
Does I_2(T) form a regular sequence for every product of linear forms T?
```

No permanent-side computation is authorized until this structural question is
proved or an exact counterexample is found.
