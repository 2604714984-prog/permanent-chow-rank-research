# Programs X and E — cross-packet principles, exact evidence, audit

## X-01 — compare endpoint complexes

Identify common algebraic structure behind the B1 target coefficient complex
and Packet-A labelled target operator.

## X-02 — permanent-gradient integrability

Seek a theorem for any 49-term endpoint packet whose sixth-derivative span
contains the permanent gradient, retaining Chow factorization and labels.

## X-03 — apolar-resolution obstruction

Use low-degree permanent apolar generators/syzygies to build one exact test
satisfied by an identity but violated by endpoint survivor models.

## X-04 — relation support versus factor span

Relate small fifth-power relation defect to the 49-dimensional factor-span
packet. A theorem excluding both packets has priority over case expansion.

## X-05 — falsification gate

A cross-packet invariant is retained only if it survives the 64-term identity,
is not contradicted by known exact controls, and gives a computable nonzero
defect on an open endpoint class.

## X-06 — promotion use

A shared theorem may replace separate B2/A work only after its hypotheses are
derived from the endpoint reduction for both packets.

## E-01 — hypothesis ledger

For every theorem-facing result record field, characteristic, ordinary/border
scope, reducedness, distinctness, nonzero weights, minimality, rank stratum,
boundary coverage, and external dependencies.

## E-02 — modular discovery discipline

Finite fields may locate components, pivots, candidate minors, or survivors.
They do not certify characteristic-zero nonexistence.

## E-03 — accepted certificate forms

Load-bearing results end as exact rational elimination, exact integer
determinant/identity, valid modular lift, controlled number-field certificate,
or exact combinatorial enumeration with independent replay.

## E-04 — survivor serialization

Store one compact payload per exact survivor. Discard nonessential search
dumps after extracting theorem-relevant coordinates.

## E-05 — semicontinuity audit

For every degeneration record rank direction, kernel direction, target
closedness, nonzero-weight survival, reducedness, and flatness of sums.

## E-06 — negative controls

Tests must fail if term weights, target rows, mixed partials, point
distinctness, reducedness, labels, or boundary charts are omitted.

## E-07 — independent proof-chain review

Before promotion, audit from lower 49 through the two endpoint packets without
assuming the intended lemma order.

## E-08 — artifact cap

Prefer one proof note, one compact payload, one replay, and focused tests per
final computational lemma. Do not build a generalized experiment platform.
