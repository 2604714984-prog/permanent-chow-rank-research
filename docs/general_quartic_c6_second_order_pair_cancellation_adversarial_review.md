# Adversarial review: canonical `C6` second-order pair cancellation

## Could another source mode cancel the boundary tangent monomial?

No. A boundary tangent from `Q_v` contains a three-cell subset of `Q_v` plus
one cell outside the fixed `3 x 3` block. A second source mode would have to
contain the same three internal cells. Distinct cross modes intersect in at
most two cells. Multiplicity-one sources have zero order-zero coefficient.

## Could source-coefficient jets create the eighteen cross-boundary matchings?

No. All initial source monomials are internal. A source jet creates no new
variable, and a mixed source-factor term or factor acceleration creates at
most one boundary variable. Each of the eighteen target matchings not fixing
`(3,3)` contains two boundary variables.

## Does equality of first factor velocities follow only modulo gauge?

Equation (2.2) is coefficientwise in a coordinate quartic monomial with one
boundary cell. Scaling gauges affect the coefficient of the original factor
cell, not an independent boundary cell. Hence every boundary coefficient is
equal literally, which is all the quadratic cancellation needs.

## Are the two endpoint source coefficients necessarily opposite?

Yes. `Q_v` occurs in exactly two source coordinates and the total order-zero
coefficient of that coordinate monomial is zero. The source map is injective
inside each canonical frame because its six factor cells are distinct.

## Does the theorem exclude all six-frame `C6` covers?

No. It treats the canonical six frames obtained from all six complements of
perfect matchings in one fixed `3 x 3` block. Other equality-frame covers may
use different omitted rows and columns and can have different source
multiplicity and tangent-collision geometry.

## Strict verdict

The canonical raw support cover is a false positive and is excluded at
coefficient level. The unrestricted six-block problem, general coordinate
second-order covers, singular degenerations, and noncoordinate initial frames
remain open. No ordinary or border Chow-rank improvement is claimed.
