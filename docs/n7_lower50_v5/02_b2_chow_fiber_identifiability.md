# v5 Module 02 — Packet-B Chow-fiber identifiability

## B2I-01 — Define the gauged 49-term Chow parameter space

Write the factor-line, coefficient, quotient-frame, and graph-map parameters
for Packet B modulo product-preserving factor rescalings and finite factor
permutations.  Separate the seven rank-six normal forms from the 42
rank-seven complements.


**Decisive output:** A smooth-chart dimension table and explicit gauge action.

## B2I-02 — Write the exact Chow-sum tangent map

Differentiate the full degree-seven Chow-sum parameterization without
projecting to `U`-degree layers.  Stream monomial support or use target weights;
do not materialize `Sym^7(k^49)`.


**Decisive output:** A basis-independent tangent formula and bounded evaluator.

## B2I-03 — Identify the row-torus orbit directions

Compute the connected row-torus tangent directions in the parameter space and
their images under the gauge quotient.  State when an orbit direction is
already an allowed factor rescaling.


**Decisive output:** Exact torus-orbit tangent subspace.

## B2I-04 — Couple the tangent map to the intrinsic `B/C` complex

Differentiate the minimal local middle factorizations and the endpoint
condition simultaneously.  A tangent vector must preserve both the permanent
identity and zero coupling defect.


**Decisive output:** A joint first-order deformation complex.

## B2I-05 — Use cubic-apolar surjectivity at first order

Differentiate
`I3(perm7) -> ker B` and express the obstruction to preserving surjectivity.
Determine which factor-frame tangent directions are killed by the apolar
evaluation rather than by the polynomial identity.


**Decisive output:** An exact first-order apolar rigidity map.

## B2I-06 — Compute the tangent endomorphism centralizer

Compare tangent automorphisms of the Chow parameterization with the
multi-degree centralizer from Module 01.  Identify non-gauge directions which
preserve all framed maps to first order.


**Decisive output:** A finite tangent-survivor space.

## B2I-07 — Formulate the local quasi-finiteness criterion

Give a precise criterion under which the fiber of the Chow-sum map is locally
quasi-finite along the connected row-torus orbit after gauges.  Include
reducedness or obstruction-space hypotheses needed to integrate tangent
statements.


**Decisive output:** A theorem usable to recover row eigenlines.

## B2I-08 — Apply the Hessian/tangential weak-defectivity test

Construct the second fundamental form on the tangent-kernel directions that
survive the first-order gates.  Use torus blocks and the special permanent
target rather than a generic secant-variety dimension count.


**Decisive output:** A second-order obstruction matrix or an exact null component.

## B2I-09 — Revisit the noncanonical `U1` tangent survivor

Integrate the exact factor-0 all-four-direction branch beyond second order

**Decisive output:** `NONCANONICAL-BRANCH-OBSTRUCTED` or an exact formal/finite survivor.

## B2I-10 — Audit canonical two-transposition joins under joint deformation

Allow both original transposition slices and the repairing term to move in the
joint tangent complex.  The previous fixed-family obstruction is an input, not
the conclusion.


**Decisive output:** A complete minimal five-term deformation decision.

## B2I-11 — Classify row-torus stabilizer jumps

Determine the components where the connected row torus has additional

**Decisive output:** A finite exceptional stabilizer list.

## B2I-12 — Use exact slice survivors as falsifiers

The one-transposition survivor must survive every condition it genuinely
satisfies and fail only at a global/full-packet gate.  This prevents local
identifiability assumptions from being smuggled into the proof.


**Decisive output:** An adversarial regression suite.

## B2I-13 — Prove local identifiability on the main equality component

Combine the tangent, apolar, and second-order maps to prove that the row-torus
orbit is gauge-trivial on the main component, or produce a positive-dimensional
exact component.


**Decisive output:** `MAIN-COMPONENT-LOCALLY-IDENTIFIABLE` or an exact family.

## B2I-14 — Integrate exceptional tangent components

For each exceptional component from task 11, determine whether it integrates
to a finite Chow fiber, a positive-dimensional fiber, or no actual permanent
identity.


**Decisive output:** A complete component table.

## B2I-15 — Extract frame/block synchronization

Apply local identifiability to show that connected row-torus motion preserves
each Chow term, yielding monomial quotient frames and row-block graph support.
Do not include projective-tail equality in this theorem.


**Decisive output:** `B2-FRAMES-AND-BLOCKS-RECOVERED`.

## B2I-16 — Decide the identifiability route

If local identifiability fails, freeze the smallest exact complete equality
component showing failure.  Projected or slice-only examples are insufficient.


**Decisive output:** `B2-IDENTIFIABILITY-CLOSED` or `B2-IDENTIFIABILITY-SURVIVOR`.
