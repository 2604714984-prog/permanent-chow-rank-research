# v5 Module 06 — Packet-A cross-block Pluecker compatibility

## AP-01 — Write the seven forced-gradient blocks in common coordinates

For each omitted column, retain the coefficient transport determined by the
same 49 factor matrices and term weights.  Do not allow independent target
coefficients in different blocks.


**Decisive output:** One cross-block gradient tensor.

## AP-02 — Derive termwise Pluecker relations among block transports

For one seven-factor term, eliminate factor rescalings and derive the quadratic
minors linking its seven omitted-column coefficient blocks.


**Decisive output:** A finite ideal of termwise cross-block relations.

## AP-03 — Add forced-Hessian compatibility

Express the 21 omitted-column-pair Hessian transports as polarizations of the
same cross-block gradient data.  Separate derivative tautologies from new
nonlinear equations.


**Decisive output:** A minimal non-tautological Hessian block.

## AP-04 — Remove the automatic `2/5` and `3/4` directions

Quotient every cross-block equation by the images proven automatic in the two
route-exhaustion theorems.  The residual must vanish on Glynn64 but need not
vanish for an arbitrary catalectic factorization.


**Decisive output:** A genuinely new residual operator.

## AP-05 — Construct the factor-plane Pluecker map

Associate each factor seven-plane with its decomposable Pluecker coordinate
and express the forced-gradient transport through contractions of this
coordinate.  Track the simple-matroid open conditions.


**Decisive output:** A basis-independent Pluecker formulation.

## AP-06 — Compute the permanent cross-block target

Evaluate the residual operator on `perm_7` by row/column weights.  Identify the
smallest nonzero target block.


**Decisive output:** Exact target block and rank.

## AP-07 — Compute the one-term nonlinear cap

Determine the dimension/rank contribution of one product term subject to its
own Pluecker relations.  Linearizing independent block coefficients is not
allowed.


**Decisive output:** A sharp one-term cap.

## AP-08 — Derive cross-weight commutator identities

Compare two omitted-column blocks transported around a third column.  Compute
commutators or cocycles that must vanish for one common factor matrix but are
not forced by separate target containment.


**Decisive output:** A small exact cross-weight invariant.

## AP-09 — Use `W^all != 0` constructively

Rather than pairing Hessian witnesses through the tautological channel, use
their termwise supports to select a nonzero cross-block Pluecker or commutator
component.


**Decisive output:** A non-tautological witness lemma.

## AP-10 — Incorporate `K2,K5 != 0` only through nonlinear incidence

The dimensions alone are exhausted.  Use the existence of relations to force
a rank drop or singularity in the shared factor-coordinate incidence scheme.


**Decisive output:** A nonlinear relation-to-defect theorem.

## AP-11 — Analyze the simple-matroid main chart

Choose a bounded set of invertible minors defining a main chart of pairwise
transverse factor planes.  Prove the residual cross-block equations have no
solution or positive codimension incompatible with the target.


**Decisive output:** A main-chart exclusion or exact survivor.

## AP-12 — Cover chart boundaries

Every vanishing minor introduced by the main chart must map to a
theorem-defined factor-plane incidence branch.  Use flat limits or another
chart; do not appeal to genericity.


**Decisive output:** A boundary-complete proof.

## AP-13 — Use the 49-term Glynn truncation as a negative control

Recover its exact residual rank 35 or a stronger cross-block defect.  The
calculation must use the same operator as the theorem.


**Decisive output:** Exact negative control.

## AP-14 — Use Glynn64 as a positive control

Verify zero residual with the actual coefficients and factor transports.
This prevents an inconsistent cross-block system.


**Decisive output:** Exact positive control.

## AP-15 — Extract the cross-block theorem

State the minimum permanent-specific nonlinear compatibility which every
49-term Packet-A equality point violates.


**Decisive output:** `PACKET-A-CROSS-BLOCK-DEFECT` or one exact zero-defect component.
