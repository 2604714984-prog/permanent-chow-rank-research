# v5 Module 03 — Packet-B projective-tail moment rigidity

## B2T-01 — Define row-split tail matrices

After frame/block synchronization, write each graph term as
`prod_a(q_a+u_(t,a))`, identify `U_a` with a six-space `W`, and collect the
seven tails into a `7 x 6` matrix `Z_t`.  The common-code condition is
`rank Z_t=1` with no zero row.


**Decisive output:** One invariant chart definition and its boundary charts.

## B2T-02 — Write every `U`-degree moment tensor

For degrees two through seven, express the permanent coefficient layers as
sums of rank-one tensors in the rows of the `Z_t`, with the same 42 term
weights in every layer.


**Decisive output:** Exact formulas indexed by row subsets and permutation cycle type.

## B2T-03 — Isolate the transposition moment system

Write the 21 `U2 Q5` equations as simultaneous bilinear moments.  Retain the
identity weights and all cross-term intersections; do not decompose into
independent local slices.


**Decisive output:** A small exact bilinear operator.

## B2T-04 — Add the three-cycle moment system

Write the 70 `U3 Q4` targets and their cyclic orientations.  Determine the
cocycle relations linking the transposition and three-cycle moments.


**Decisive output:** A coupled degree-2/3 moment complex.

## B2T-05 — Add four-cycle and two-transposition targets

Use the 315 `U4 Q3` monomials to distinguish genuine common tails from
pairwise-compatible but globally inconsistent row tails.


**Decisive output:** A minimal degree-4 compatibility block.

## B2T-06 — Form the tail-minor ideal

Let `I_tail` be generated by all two-by-two minors of every `Z_t`.  State the
full equality ideal `J_eq` after frame/block recovery, including moment
equations, apolar surjectivity, nonzero weights, and reducedness.


**Decisive output:** The exact radical-containment target `I_tail subset sqrt(J_eq)`.

## B2T-07 — Derive linearized tail-minor consequences

Compute the tangent image of the tail minors on every row-split equality
component.  Identify components where rank-one follows infinitesimally and
where higher-order equations are required.


**Decisive output:** A finite tangent branch list.

## B2T-08 — Group six-row layers into three-way tensors

For each omitted row, group the six active row factors as `2+2+2`, obtaining a
`36 x 36 x 36` tensor with at most 42 shared rank-one summands.  Compute exact
flattening and compound ranks of the permanent target.


**Decisive output:** A symmetry-reduced tensor package.

## B2T-09 — Test Kruskal/simultaneous-decomposition identifiability

Determine whether the grouped factor matrices satisfy a provable Kruskal or
compound-matrix uniqueness criterion on the main component.  The theorem must
cover the special target, not only generic tensors.


**Decisive output:** `MAIN-TAIL-DECOMPOSITION-IDENTIFIABLE` or a rank-deficient exception.

## B2T-10 — Classify rank-deficient grouped factors

When a Kruskal condition fails, stratify by exact rank drops, common kernels,
and support partitions.  Use the existing high-overlap certificate library
only when its formal hypotheses match.


**Decisive output:** A finite rank-deficient branch inventory.

## B2T-11 — Couple the seven omitted-row decompositions

The seven six-row tensors share the same 42 graph terms.  Prove compatibility
of their component labellings and weights, eliminating independent
permutations between omitted-row blocks.


**Decisive output:** A simultaneous component-matching theorem.

## B2T-12 — Use cubic-apolar surjectivity on tail moments

Translate the global apolar-surjectivity condition into injectivity constraints
on the dual moment arrays.  Determine which rank-deficient branches are
incompatible with the 19600-dimensional source.


**Decisive output:** An apolar tail-rigidity lemma.

## B2T-13 — Derive cycle-ratio consistency

For nonzero tails, compare ratios inferred from transpositions, three-cycles,
and four-cycles.  Prove that all row tails of one term are proportional, or
isolate the precise cocycle obstruction.


**Decisive output:** A cross-row ratio theorem.

## B2T-14 — Handle zero-tail and repeated-tail boundaries

Cover zero rows, coincident graph terms, zero combined coefficients,
nonreduced limits, and projective chart changes.  A zero tail must not be
assigned an artificial projective point.


**Decisive output:** A flat boundary theorem.

## B2T-15 — Use the one-transposition survivor as a negative control

Verify that the local survivor passes the degree-2 slice but fails the first
global simultaneous-moment or component-matching gate.  The failure must be
mathematically identified, not hard-coded.


**Decisive output:** One exact adversarial control.

## B2T-16 — Use common-graph packets as positive controls

Every legal common-tail configuration must satisfy the tail-minor theorem and
map to the already closed B1 code.  This tests orientations and coefficient
transport.


**Decisive output:** A positive-control suite.

## B2T-17 — Prove the tail radical containment

Combine the main and exceptional branches to prove
`I_tail subset sqrt(J_eq)` on every row-split equality component, or freeze one
exact non-rank-one equality survivor.


**Decisive output:** `B2-TAILS-SYNCHRONIZED` or `B2-TAIL-SURVIVOR`.

## B2T-18 — Transfer to B1 and close Packet B

Once each tail matrix has rank one, construct the shared point code with the
correct nonzero term weights and invoke `B1-CLOSED`.  Verify that every
hypothesis of B1 is inherited.


**Decisive output:** `B2-CLOSED` or one explicit transfer gap.
