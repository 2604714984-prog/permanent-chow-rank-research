# Ledger delta: squarefree quotient Koszul homology

## New exact theorem

For the independent `n`-factor Chow term and every rank-`d` factor quotient,

\[
\max\dim H^1_{n,k}=d\binom{n-d}{k-1}.
\]

Adjacent and all-positive-degree caps are

\[
d\binom{n-d+1}{k}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
d2^{n-d}.
\]

## Research decision

The first genuine cross-degree relation quotient has a closed one-term profile
for independent factors.  The next task is to determine whether dependent or
repeated factors violate that cap.
