# N6-120: the identity \(K_{3,2}\) graph germ

**Status.** PURE_FORMAL_LOCAL_K32_GRAPH_GERM. This is a characteristic-zero
local theorem for the rank-at-most-six graph incidence.

Let \(A=k^3\), let \(P,Q\) be two-dimensional column spaces, and write a
general graph operator as
\[
T:A\otimes P\longrightarrow A\otimes Q.
\]
In the ambient \(A\otimes(P\oplus Q)\), set
\[
L=\operatorname{graph}(T),\qquad M=\operatorname{graph}(-T).
\]
At \(T=I_6\), the rectangle cross matrix has size \(36\times18\) and exact
rank six. Choose its six pivot rows and columns. In the corresponding Schur
chart, the derivative of the \(12\times12\) Schur complement has \(36\)
columns, one for each entry of \(T\).

The exact rational calculation gives
\[
\operatorname{rank}(J)=34,\qquad \dim\ker J=2.
\]
The kernel is generated by simultaneous scaling of the three entries in
source column zero and simultaneous scaling of the three entries in source
column one:
\[
\delta T=\operatorname{diag}(s,t,s,t,s,t).
\]
After taking \(T[0,0]\) and \(T[1,1]\) as the two free coordinates, the
remaining \(34\times34\) Jacobian minor has determinant
\[
-256\ne0.
\]

The two-parameter family
\[
T(s,t)=\operatorname{diag}(s,t,s,t,s,t)
\]
has identically zero Schur complement in this chart; its pivot determinant is
\(-8t^3\), hence it remains in the chart near \((s,t)=(1,1)\). The formal
implicit-function theorem therefore gives:

> In the completed local ring at \(T=I_6\), the rank-at-most-six graph
> incidence is exactly the two-parameter matching family
> \(T=\operatorname{diag}(s,t,s,t,s,t)\).

The row-column torus preserving \(S_0(A)\otimes S_0(B)\) sends \(T=I_6\) to
exactly this family: row factors cancel, while the two source-to-target column
ratios give \(s\) and \(t\). The family is therefore a two-dimensional torus
orbit. Since the completed local incidence has dimension two, the irreducible
component through \(T=I_6\) inside the invertible graph chart is exactly this
matching orbit.

This is stronger than a tangent-space statement. Every nearby
rank-at-most-six complementary graph pair in this chart preserves the
\(2+2\) column matching. It is still only a local theorem: it does not
classify arbitrary invertible \(6\times6\) graph operators globally, lower-rank
collision points, or the full six-term Chow cocycle. It does not by itself
prove ordinary lower 29 or exact unrestricted Chow rank 32.
