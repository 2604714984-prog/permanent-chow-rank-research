# v7 Module 10 — exact elimination and survivor protocol

Computation begins only after theorem-level reduction and must distinguish projected controls from complete 50-term identities.

## EX-01 — define exact branch equations

After structural reduction, write the smallest polynomial system for each branch with coefficients shared across degrees.

**Decisive output:** `BRANCH-EQUATIONS`.

## EX-02 — fix gauge and projective redundancies

Remove only symmetries proved to preserve the full identity and all target equations.

**Decisive output:** `EXACT-GAUGE`.

## EX-03 — saturate nonzero coefficients

Exclude zero Chow coefficients and collapsed factors exactly.

**Decisive output:** `NONZERO-SATURATION`.

## EX-04 — cover rank charts

Every invertible-minor chart must have an explicit complement or another theorem.

**Decisive output:** `CHART-COVERAGE`.

## EX-05 — use modular discovery only diagnostically

Finite fields may locate components, pivots, and minors but never certify characteristic-zero nonexistence alone.

**Decisive output:** `MODULAR-DISCOVERY-RULE`.

## EX-06 — promote to characteristic zero

Use rational elimination, integer minors, or exact number-field points with minimal polynomials.

**Decisive output:** `CHAR0-CERTIFICATES`.

## EX-07 — serialize structural survivors

Store factor planes, local algebras, residual modules, and all claimed ranks.

**Decisive output:** `STRUCTURAL-SURVIVOR-PAYLOAD`.

## EX-08 — upgrade to full identity candidates

Solve for actual seven-factor products and coefficients on each structural survivor.

**Decisive output:** `FULL-IDENTITY-CANDIDATES`.

## EX-09 — verify the polynomial residual

Expand or evaluate deterministically enough to prove the candidate sum equals the permanent exactly.

**Decisive output:** `IDENTITY-RESIDUAL-CHECK`.

## EX-10 — verify minimality

Confirm all 50 coefficients are nonzero and no proper subpacket cancels.

**Decisive output:** `SURVIVOR-MINIMALITY`.

## EX-11 — independent survivor replay

Use a separate implementation and arithmetic domain.

**Decisive output:** `SURVIVOR-INDEPENDENT`.

## EX-12 — prioritize genuine survivors

A full identity suspends exclusion work and receives immediate adversarial verification.

**Decisive output:** `SURVIVOR-PRIORITY`.

## EX-13 — close finite subcases

Every theorem-defined finite branch ends in a certificate or survivor, not an enlarged search box.

**Decisive output:** `FINITE-BRANCHES-DECIDED`.

## EX-14 — publish the exact frontier

Return a complete table of closed branches and any surviving full identities.

**Decisive output:** `EXACT-FRONTIER`.
