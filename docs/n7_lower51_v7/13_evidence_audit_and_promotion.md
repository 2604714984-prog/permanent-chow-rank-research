Include only lemmas actually used to exclude all 50-term packets.

**Decisive output:** `L51-DEPENDENCY-GRAPH`.

## EV-02 — freeze exact heads and trees

Record every proof and computational dependency at immutable commits.

**Decisive output:** `L51-HEADS-FROZEN`.

**Decisive output:** `L51-EVIDENCE-MANIFEST`.

**Decisive output:** `L51-SOURCE-RECONCILIATION`.

**Decisive output:** `L51-INDEPENDENT-REPLAYS`.

**Decisive output:** `L51-MATH-AUDIT`.

**Decisive output:** `L51-FINDINGS-LEDGER`.

**Decisive output:** `L51-REPAIR-GATE`.

**Decisive output:** `L51-CI-PASS`.
