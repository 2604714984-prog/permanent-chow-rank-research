# Ledger delta: exact squarefree quotient-symbol profile

## New exact local theorem

For an independent `n`-factor Chow term and every rank-`d` quotient of its
factor span,

\[
\min \operatorname{rank}\partial_{k,P}
=
\binom nk-\binom{n-d}{k}.
\]

The adjacent shared-quotient direct sum has the sum of the two separate
minimum ranks, and the all-positive-degree direct sum has minimum

\[
2^n-2^{n-d}.
\]

## Route decision

Sharing one factor quotient across degrees is not a coupling while the outputs
remain block diagonal.  The next object must quotient or measure the common
Koszul/commutation relations.
