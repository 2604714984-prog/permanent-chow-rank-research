# N6-093: the defect-four \(b=34,\ x_A=72\) frontier

N6-091 gives \(x_A,f_A\le72\) for every residual seven-set. At equality,
N6-092 gives

\[
 \dim X=72,\qquad \dim\partial X=89,\qquad
 \dim\partial^2X=24.                                         \tag{1.1}
\]

The seven-term quadratic projection cap remains 93, so the defect budget is
four. Exact enumeration of the omitted-factor, positive-\(\varepsilon\)
factor-span, relation-kernel, and conservative middle-rank inequalities gives
21 scalar states. Existing \(t_2\le14\) prolongation bounds exclude 18. Three
actual packets remain.

## 1. Direct \(t_2=16\)

\[
 \varepsilon=(0^7),\qquad\kappa_2=0,\qquad
 (d_2,a_2,t_2)=(105,\ge89,\le16).                            \tag{1.2}
\]

The seven quadratic and middle images are literal direct, so the required
prolongation dimension is

\[
 400+140-72=468.                                             \tag{1.3}
\]

The known cap 458 applies only after one forces \(t_2\le15\); it cannot be
used here without a new argument.

## 2. One quadratic relation over common \(W_{15}\)

\[
 \varepsilon=(0^7),\qquad\kappa_2=1,\qquad
 (d_2,a_2,t_2)=(104,89,15).                                  \tag{1.4}
\]

The required lower bound is 466. Any epsilon-zero term with
\(\alpha\le2\) is excluded by the cap 458. Thus all seven alpha values are
three and their quotient images equal the same \(W_{15}\). The remaining
issue is to combine this actual common-quotient packet with the missing
quadratic cell in N6-092.

## 3. One defective term

\[
 \varepsilon=(0,0,0,0,0,0,1),\qquad\kappa_2=0,\qquad
 (d_2,a_2,t_2)=(104,89,15).                                  \tag{1.5}
\]

The six epsilon-zero terms are forced to have alpha three and quotient image
\(W_{15}\). The positive-epsilon term has alpha at least two and quotient
dimension 13, 14, or 15. The required prolongation lower bound is 468. This packet
needs a coupled treatment of the six common-quotient terms and the one
defective frame.

These are genuine open packets, not exclusions. N6-092 shows that their
89-plane shadow is a hyperplane in a partitioned product \(90\)-plane and
has second shadow 24, but its extra Boolean branches need not transport
actual frames. The next target is the one-relation common-\(W_{15}\) packet.
This result does not exclude \(x_A=72\) or global \(b=34\), prove ordinary
lower \(29\), or imply a border-rank bound.
