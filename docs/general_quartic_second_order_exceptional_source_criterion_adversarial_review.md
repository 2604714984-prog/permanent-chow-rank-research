# Adversarial review: exceptional-source criterion

The criterion deliberately concerns a target outside every one-factor
envelope. Without that hypothesis, source jets, mixed source-factor terms, or
factor accelerations may contribute and pair cancellation is not exhaustive.

Condition 3 is necessary: an intermediate tangent monomial may be canceled by
a first-order source coefficient even when no other order-zero source is
active. Condition 4 separately covers tangent cancellation from another active
source. These two mechanisms must not be conflated.

Repeated factors can produce several labeled source branches over the same
specialized monomial and internal kernel tangents; condition 1 excludes that
case and records it as `INTERNAL_FIBER` rather than silently assuming
injectivity.

The four exceptions are necessary mechanisms, not sufficient constructions.
The theorem gives no unrestricted six-block zero statement, ordinary-rank or
border-rank improvement, or literature-novelty claim.
