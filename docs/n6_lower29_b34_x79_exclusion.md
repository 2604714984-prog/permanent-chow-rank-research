# N6-085: the \(b=34\) seven-set shortening reaches \(78\)

## 1. Improved literal floors

N6-083 leaves \(f_A\le79\) for every residual seven-set \(A\). With
\(\dim S\ge366\), every residual fifteen-set \(B\), whose complement has
seven elements, satisfies

\[
 \dim L_B\ge366-79=287.
\]

Enlarging a seven-set to a fifteen-set and removing the other eight terms
gives

\[
 \dim L_A\ge287-8\cdot20=127.                                 \tag{1.1}
\]

The product-shadow jump \(m_{81}=96>93\) still gives \(x_A\le80\).

## 2. Excluding \(x_A=80\) and \(x_A=79\)

For either value, the exact first shadow is \(90\), so the seven-term
omitted-factor defect is at most three. The same complete relation envelope
as N6-081 has eleven states. Existing \(t_2\le14\) caps exclude ten. The
sole survivor is

\[
 \varepsilon=(0^7),\qquad\kappa_2=0,\qquad d_2=105,\qquad t_2=15.
 \tag{2.1}
\]

Quadratic directness makes the seven middle spaces literal direct, so
\(\dim L_A=140\). If any term had \(\alpha\le2\), the \(t_2=15\) cap would
be \(458\), while

\[
 400+140-x=
 \begin{cases}
 460,&x=80,\\
 461,&x=79.
 \end{cases}                                                   \tag{2.2}
\]

Thus every \(\alpha_i=3\), the quotient images are one common \(W_{15}\),
and \(a_2=90\).

For \(x=80\), N6-082 supplies the partitioned \(4\times6\) second shadow.
For \(x=79\), N6-084 extends the plane to an \(80\to90\) plane with the same
\(K\), hence supplies the same shadow. N6-083's actual-frame proof depends
only on this product \(K\), the seven direct common-\(W_{15}\) frames, and
the fact that the central intersection is larger than \(60\). Its
invertible-block branch has intersection at most \(60\), and its all-singular
branch cannot contain a rank-six factor frame. Both values are impossible.

## 3. Conclusion and boundary

Every global \(b=34\) survivor now satisfies

\[
 \boxed{x_A\le78,\qquad f_A\le78}
\]

for every residual seven-set. The \(78\to90\) locus is not classified here.
This does not exclude global \(b=34\), prove lower 29, or make a border-rank
claim.
