# N6-087: excluding the \(x_A=78\) shortening layer

N6-085 leaves \(x_A,f_A\le78\) for every residual seven-set. Therefore every
complementary fifteen-set has literal dimension at least

\[
 366-78=288,
\]

and every seven-set has literal dimension at least

\[
 288-8\cdot20=128.                                             \tag{1.1}
\]

Assume \(x_A=78\). Its exact product shadow is \(90\), so the same
defect-three relation envelope has eleven states. Existing \(t_2\le14\)
caps exclude ten. The survivor is

\[
 \varepsilon=(0^7),\qquad\kappa_2=0,\qquad d_2=105,\qquad t_2=15.
\]

Quadratic directness gives seven literal-direct middle spaces and
\(\dim L_A=140\). Any \(\alpha_i\le2\) would give the \(458\) cap, whereas

\[
 \dim(E_3+L_A)=400+140-78=462.                                \tag{1.2}
\]

Thus all \(\alpha_i=3\), the quotient images are one common \(W_{15}\), and
\(a_2=90\). N6-086 extends this \(78\to90\) plane to the partitioned
\(80\to90\) product locus with the same quadratic shadow. N6-083's block
dichotomy applies unchanged: its invertible-block branch has cubic
permanent intersection at most \(60<78\), and its all-singular branch cannot
support a rank-six frame in four active rows. Hence \(x_A=78\) is impossible.

Therefore every global \(b=34\) survivor satisfies

\[
 \boxed{x_A\le77,\qquad f_A\le77}
\]

for every residual seven-set. The \(77\to90\) equality locus remains open.
This does not exclude global \(b=34\), prove lower 29, or make a border-rank
claim.
