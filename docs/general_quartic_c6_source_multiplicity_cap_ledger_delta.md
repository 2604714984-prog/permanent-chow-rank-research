# Ledger delta: `C6` source-multiplicity cap

For distinct quartic second-order equality-frame types, every four-edge source
monomial occurs in at most two frames. The three source shapes and extension
counts are

```text
P5                 2
P4 disjoint P2     1
P3 disjoint P3     2
```

Across all 96 labeled equality frames there are 1,008 distinct source
monomials: 576 have multiplicity one and 432 have multiplicity two. None has
multiplicity at least three.

Thus a `TRIPLE_SOURCE` exceptional mechanism requires repeated frame copies.
For distinct equality frames, only source-jet absorption and tangent collision
remain as second-order exceptions. `mu(6,4)` remains open in `[6,8]`.
