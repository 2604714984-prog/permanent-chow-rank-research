## 2. Defect variables

For the four fixed terms put

\[
G_i=\mathcal D_2(T_i),
\]

and define

\[
\varepsilon_i
=15-\dim G_i,
\qquad
\alpha_i
=3-\dim(E_2\cap G_i).
\tag{2.1}
\]

Both are nonnegative. Apply the omitted-factor projection proof with the `j`th factor hidden. It gives

\[
\dim\partial S
\le
\sum_{i\ne j}\dim G_i
+
\dim(E_2\cap G_j).
\]

Combining this with (1.1) yields:

### Proposition 2.1 — per-omitted-factor defect budget

For every `j=1,2,3,4`,

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i\ne j}\varepsilon_i+\alpha_j
\le
27-b.
}
\tag{2.2}
\]

Summing the four inequalities gives

\[
\boxed{
3\sum_i\varepsilon_i+\sum_i\alpha_i
\le
4(27-b).
}
\tag{2.3}
\]

## 3. The excluded endpoint `b=27`

At `b=27`, equation (2.2) forces

\[

\qquad

\[

\qquad
\dim(E_2\cap G_i)=3,
\qquad

Equality in every omitted-factor projection has a stronger consequence. A section of

\[

The sum-zero condition in `Sym^2 V/E_2` forces the four 12-dimensional quotient images to coincide.

is a direct sum of dimension 60. Differentiating any relation among the four cubic derivative spaces then proves that those spaces are direct as well. Symmetry of the middle catalectics gives coupled central rank

\[

The 19-term residual inequality gives instead

\[

Therefore `b=27` is impossible. The current range is

\tag{3.1}

## 4. The new top layer `b=26`

At `b=26`, equation (2.2) becomes

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i\ne j}\varepsilon_i+\alpha_j
\le1
\qquad(j=1,2,3,4).
}
\tag{4.1}
\]

The total weighted defect obeys

\[
3\sum_i\varepsilon_i+\sum_i\alpha_i\le4.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Several immediate consequences are useful.

1. At most one `epsilon_i` can be nonzero.
2. If some `epsilon_i=1`, then every other `epsilon` and every `alpha` is zero.
3. If all `epsilon_i=0`, then each `alpha_i` is zero or one, with no further restriction from (4.1) alone.
4. Every factor with `alpha_i=0` lies in the equality locus
   \[
   \dim(E_2\cap\operatorname{Sym}^2L_i)=3
   \]
   and is therefore governed by the disjoint-support `2 x 3` / `3 x 2` classification.

Thus the `b=26` layer has only two defect families:

```text
A. one quadratic-dimension defect epsilon_i=1; all other defects zero;
B. all quadratic dimensions maximal; an arbitrary subset of alpha_i equals 1.
```

This is a finite structural split, not a broad Grassmannian search.
